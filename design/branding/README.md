# MOA brand assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-20. Both outputs
were visually inspected for composition and Korean text. The opaque icon uses
the site's coral/cream palette and interlocking conversation bubbles. The OG
image matches the existing clay-style community illustration.

Originals: `moa-icon-source-v1.png`, `moa-og-source-v1.png`.
Run `powershell -File scripts/export-brand-assets.ps1` from the repository root
to export browser icon sizes and `frontend/public/images/moa-og-v1.jpg` (1200x630).
Exporting resizes/encodes the generated originals; it does not redraw artwork.

## Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production favicon/app icon for Korean community service '모아' (MOA), where people connect, share ideas and grow. Create ONE square 1024x1024 raster icon, front-facing and centered. Existing website brand uses warm cream #fbfaf8, coral terracotta #c36f51, soft clay/jelly 3D illustrations. Fill the entire canvas with a saturated warm coral background, no outer white margin, no device mockup. Center a single bold warm-ivory symbol made of two friendly interlocking rounded speech bubbles, suggesting conversation and togetherness, with a subtly sculpted soft jelly surface, minimal shallow depth. The symbol occupies about 70% of canvas, strong readable silhouette at 16 pixels. Each bubble must be simple, large and chunky; no tiny dots or linework. Premium, warm and friendly, understated. No text, no letters, no watermark, no extra objects, no gradients that reduce contrast. Full opaque background.

## OG prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: final Open Graph social sharing image for Korean community website 모아, to display in KakaoTalk link previews. Produce ONE wide landscape image with exact 1200x630 composition (aspect ratio 1.90476:1). Warm ivory #fbfaf8 full-bleed background, coral terracotta #c36f51, pastel sage, peach and lavender accents, dark warm charcoal typography. Refined friendly Korean community brand, gentle clay/jelly 3D sculptural style matching a warm modern community website. Left half: beautifully typeset very large Korean wordmark exactly '모아.' with coral-colored final dot. Below it, generous two-line Korean headline, exactly '생각을 나누고,' on the first line and '함께 성장하는 공간' on second line. Lower left small clean domain text exactly 'onboardproject.shop'. Right half: polished cluster of two oversized soft 3D conversation bubbles, a coral heart, and two tiny friendly faceless community avatar medallions, one peach and one sage, representing sharing and togetherness. Composition feels balanced, airy, editorial, premium and welcoming, not busy. All text sharply readable and correctly spelled, entirely inside a 70-pixel safe margin. Important wordmark and main objects survive thumbnail cropping. Soft natural studio lighting and delicate shadows, no dark vignette, no black background. No browser UI, no photo mockup, no watermark, no additional text.
